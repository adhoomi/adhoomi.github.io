Mini Mario

Explore real-time obstacle navigation, character progression, and objective completion within the Mushroom Kingdom

Game Overview 🍄

Mini Mario is a classic-style platformer game built with Python and Pygame that brings nostalgic gaming elements to modern players. The game features a hero named Mario navigating through increasingly challenging levels filled with obstacles, enemies, and collectibles. With its intuitive controls, progressive difficulty, and engaging gameplay mechanics, Mini Mario offers hours of entertainment for gamers of all ages.

The Adventure ⛳

Our hero, Mario, embarks on an exciting journey through four unique worlds, each with its own distinct environment and challenges. From grassy plains to complex aerial platforms, Mario must overcome obstacles, defeat enemies, and collect valuable coins while maintaining his limited lives. The ultimate goal: reach the invisible flag at the end of each level and progress to the next world!
