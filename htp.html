How to play!? 🎮

Movement: ⌨️

  • Move Left: ← or A
  • Move Right: → or D
  • Jump: ↑ or W
  • Jump (Alternative): Spacebar

Menu Navigation: ☰

  • Mouse: Click buttons
  • ESC: Return to Main Menu (during gameplay)

Characters & Entities:

🍄 Mario (The Hero):

  • Appearance: Cheerful red character in classic platformer style
  • Abilities:
    • Horizontal Movement: Run left and right across platforms
    • Jumping: Leap over gaps and onto higher platforms
    • Enemy Defeat: Stomp on enemies from above to eliminate them
  • Stats: Starting Lives: 3; Movement Speed: Moderate; Jump Height: High
  • Special: Can collect coins and defeat enemies

💰 Coins:

  • Purpose: Score-boosting collectibles
  • Value: 100 points each
  • Location: Strategically placed throughout levels
  • Sound: Satisfying collection chime

👾 Basic Enemies (Levels 1-2):

  • Appearance: Simple, cartoonish ground creatures
  • Behavior: Patrol back and forth; reverse at edges; slow–moderate speed
  • Interaction: Dangerous on contact; vulnerable to stomping

🟫 Platforms:

  • Types: Ground floor, elevated platforms, special floating platforms
  • Function: Provide footing, create level geometry, enable navigation

👹 Advanced Enemies (Levels 3-4):

  • Appearance: Spiky, menacing flying creatures
  • Behavior: Float at fixed heights and move horizontally; ignore platforms
  • Interaction: More dangerous due to floating; still defeatable by jumping on them

🏁 Finish Flag:

  • Feature: Invisible completion trigger
  • Location: Far right side of each level
  • Function: Ends the level when reached
  • Note: Completely invisible for added challenge
