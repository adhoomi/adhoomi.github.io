About the creator of this portfolio and projects

I’m a Mechatronics Engineering student with a passion for creating smart, intuitive systems that blend software and technology. Whether it’s programming, automation, or building interactive tools, I enjoy turning ideas into real, functional projects. This film library system was a great opportunity to expand my skills beyond hardware and dive deeper into full-stack development. Working on this project helped me connect the problem-solving mindset of engineering with the creativity of software design. I enjoyed the process of building something useful, clean, and enjoyable to interact with, and I look forward to taking on even more ambitious projects that push my technical abilities further.
